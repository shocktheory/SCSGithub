# SCS Product Charter

**Product:** ShockTheory Constitutional System (SCS)
**Parent system:** ShockTheory OS
**Product Owner:** Sonja Ross
**Primary Steward:** #SOS — Chief of Staff and Constitutional Guardian
**Version target:** v0.1 MVP · **Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only

## Purpose

Give the Product Owner **one place** to understand the full operating state of ShockTheory OS —
what it contains, what is active, what is authoritative, what changed, what is waiting, where the
risks are, and what should happen next — without searching across AI conversations.

## Governing product principle

> **One operating system. One current view. No competing sources of truth.**

SCS does not replace the constitutional documents it references. It provides the governed index,
status, relationships, and operational view of those materials, linking to the authoritative source
rather than duplicating it.

## What SCS is / is not

**Is:** an operating-system system · a constitutional index · a decision & publication tracker ·
a product portfolio view · an AI coordination surface · a living cheatsheet · a source-of-truth
navigator · a Product Owner command center.

**Is not:** a generic project manager · a GitHub replacement · a docs replacement · a task-list app ·
a static spreadsheet · a chat interface · a visual-only system · another source of constitutional truth.

## ShockTheory OS hierarchy (must be preserved, never flattened)

```
ShockTheory OS
├── SAPDOS · STACL · STP · #SOS · SCS
├── Products ── Kidlytics · CivicAI · Future Products
└── Constitutional Library
    ├── Product Architecture · Playbooks · Canonical Language
    └── Decisions · Specifications · Benchmarks
```

## Primary user

A founder-Product Owner overseeing multiple complex products, multiple AI collaborators, large
documentation libraries, constitutional decisions, architecture, design systems, builds,
publications, and IP. **The interface must reduce cognitive load, never reproduce it.**

## Non-Negotiable Rules (§38)

1. Product Owner authority remains final.
2. #SOS advises and governs; it does **not** approve on the Product Owner's behalf.
3. SCS **displays** authority; it does not manufacture it.
4. A polished interface must **not** make proposed information look approved.
5. Updates do **not** silently modify constitutional records.
6. Every consequential change requires review and confirmation.
7. Do not create competing sources of truth.
8. Do not flatten ShockTheory OS into a generic project system.
9. Do not confuse product status with publication status.
10. Do not confuse AI assignment with AI authority.
11. Do not invent data merely to make the system look complete.
12. Make unresolved information visible.
13. Preserve change history.
14. The Cheatsheet is a **generated** view, never a manually maintained duplicate.
15. Clear ships over clever.
16. Proven ships over novel unless novelty earns permanence.
17. Every build decision should leave ShockTheory OS more understandable.

## v0.1 acceptance (§37)

Success = the Product Owner understands current state in under a minute; all products and
publications have clear status; AI assignments are visible; authoritative vs. proposed is always
distinguishable; sync-code updates record; decisions persist; canonical language is governed;
benchmarks are explicit; risks are visible; the Cheatsheet is generated from live data; data backs
up and restores; it works on desktop and mobile; no invented claims are presented as approved
truth; artifacts link to their real locations; notifications can be tested and audited; SCS can be
securely deployed at shocktheoryos.com on the approved PHP/MySQL architecture.
